Soft skills

  • Problem-solving

    To streamline a repetitive task in my previous role, I developed a reconciliation application that compared transactions from two CSV files, highlighting matches in green and discrepancies in yellow. This solution improved reconciliation efficiency by 60%, making the process faster and significantly reducing errors. After my departure, I deployed the app to ensure continued use by the company. You can explore this project further on the Projects page.

  • Attention to detail

    I managed late payment charge calculations for apartment sales agreements at a construction company. To ensure accuracy, I developed a double-check process - first using a financial calculator, then verifying results against a detailed spreadsheet with fine and interest formulas. Given the complexity of values, periods, rates, and currency updates, attention to detail was critical, especially in delicate negotiations with debtors. This process improved efficiency and accuracy.

  • Time management

    With over a decade of remote work experience, I’ve mastered time management by maintaining a structured calendar and a centralized task manager, continuously refining them for efficiency. Through books and podcasts, I actively enhance my self-awareness, allowing me to identify the best times for tackling complex tasks and setting realistic daily challenges.

  • Lifelong learning

    I believe that learning has no limits - no matter your age or the complexity of the subject, persistence and dedication make growth possible. With this mindset, I continuously refine my skills, diving deep into self-study, especially as I transition to a new career.
